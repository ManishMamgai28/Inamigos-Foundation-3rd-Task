01 — ABOUT INAMIGOS

A little help can become a whole new beginning.

Founded on September 23, 2020 by Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit based in Chhattisgarh, working to create meaningful social impact across India.

From education and food support to women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental action and employability, our work focuses on both immediate needs and long-term opportunity.
